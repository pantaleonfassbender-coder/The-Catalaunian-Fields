# The Catalaunian Fields, 451

A documentary apparatus for Attila's invasion of Gaul and the battle of the Catalaunian Fields (451), and for its afterlife: public-domain sources in the original beside the English, a comparison of the chronicles, a timeline and plates. Stage 1 complete: five modules with 121 passages (Priscus, Jordanes, Prosper, Hydatius, Cassiodorus, Marcellinus, the Gallic chronicles, Sidonius, Gregory of Tours, the Lives of Anianus, Lupus and Genovefa, and the afterlife from Gibbon to 1914), nine comparisons and fourteen plates.

The companion game, *Bellum atrox*, is in preparation.

- `data/modules.json`: the modules carried, and what is not carried and why.
- `data/<module>.json`: the texts, built by the scripts in `tools/`.
- `data/timeline.json`, `data/compare.json`, `data/plates.json`.
- `tools/make-plates.py`: fetches the plates from Wikimedia Commons after checking their licence.
